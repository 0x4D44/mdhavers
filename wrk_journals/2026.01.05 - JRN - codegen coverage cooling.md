## Update: remove unreachable closure-call nil-fill loops
The remaining highest-density misses were at the defensive closure-call arg-count fill loops
(lines 31411–31419 in `src/llvm/codegen.rs`). These were effectively unreachable because
`call_args` is constructed to the exact fixed param count by design.

Change:
- Replace the two conditional nil-fill loops with an unconditional
  `call_args.resize(expected_total_param_count, self.make_nil().into())` so the “keep IR valid”
  intent remains while eliminating the uncovered loop bodies (and also safely truncating if a
  mismatch ever produces too many args).
