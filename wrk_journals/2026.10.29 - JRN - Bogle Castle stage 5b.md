# Bogle Castle stage 5b: Arthur's playtest polish

Arthur requested a red-haired HUD face, understandable pickups and scenery, an
illustrated Kit List, sgian-dubh naming and animated gamekeeper fist-shaking.
Everything is implemented in mdhavers. No Rust, C, runtime, dependency, git write,
commit or external post was made. Source PNGs and PROMPTS.md were changed only
by the concurrent art session; this code lane read and baked its completed art.

## Cause and implementation

- `games/bogle/engine/render.braw:650`: the old HUD was text only and called
  weapon 1 KNIFE. It now has a framed 32x32 red-haired face, health/spirit/ammo
  and a SGIAN-DUBH weapon strip. The 320x200 HUD is 44 pixels high; the 80x50
  parity HUD is proportionally 11 pixels high, with short numeric labels.
  Weapon selection bits and replay input format are unchanged. The sound cue
  is now `sgian_dubh`; its PCM timbre hashes remain unchanged.
- `engine/render.braw:593` and `:610`: pure face tiers use health >75 fine,
  >50 hurt, >25 bad, >0 dire, otherwise dead. Dead wins, then ouch for 8 tics,
  then grin for 18 tics after weapon/key pickups. Healthy idle glances follow
  a 140-tic cycle: left at100..113, right at120..133, using a mirrored look
  texture for right. Lower tiers keep their wounded picture because the one
  supplied glance picture is healthy. Damage/pickup markers are stored by
  `things.braw:70`/`:154`; new_game initializes them to -1000.
- `engine/render.braw:126`: pickups bob +/-2 world units in an eight-step,
  32-tic cycle and go full bright for 5 tics every70. Only projection changes;
  pickup positions, collection reach, simulation state and PRNG are untouched
  by animation. Pickup messages now state the actual effect, including the
  Girder Juice can's existing +15 health (not +25), bottle's full health and
  three seconds of double speed, shortbread's full spirit, ammunition and doors.
- `engine/things.braw:4`, `engine/render.braw:199`, `maps/e1m1.braw`: the cow
  portrait is `dec_cow_portrait`, kind34, scenery. In the checked-in map it
  was actually in the gallery at1808,240; it now hangs on the courtyard's
  south wall at192,380. The tartan banner (kind35) moves912,320 ->912,380;
  the stag head (kind30) moves624,48 ->624,4. Their bottom is floor+24 and
  their rendered height48, so their centres are48 units above the floor
  (player eye41). They retain the existing billboard projection beside walls.
  Fact: the bagpipes are a distinct kind18 pickup in the chapel. Inference:
  Arthur's 'tartan blankets' most likely meant the banner dressing, though the
  bagpipes were also an understandable source of confusion. The Kit List shows
  both weapon and pickup pictures of the pipes and explains their use.
- `engine/move.braw:89`: armour, brazier, thistle pot and curling stone now
  have16-unit furniture radii. Players and corporeal actors use the shared
  collision path, including substepped motion. Mounted dressing stays passable.
- `engine/ai.braw:32`: first sight starts stationary `fists` for35 tics,
  then the existing charge. `seen_player` prevents repeating this longer pause;
  later windups retain18 tics. Fists/windup pictures alternate every5 tics.
  Speed, attack damage, charge direction and stun timing are unchanged.
- `shell/frontend.braw:54`/`:284`, `shell/onboarding.braw:26`: Kit List is
  available from title and pause, with19 illustrated entries across10 pages:
  four weapons, eleven pickups, four enemies. Up/W goes back; Down/S or
  Enter/Space advances; Escape returns. Menu time remains frozen and held
  selection/fire suppression remains intact. Advice describes actual mechanics,
  including ghost torchlight vulnerability and bog midges/bagpipe dispersal.
  Five pause rows fit by reducing menu spacing. Door prompts move from rows
  70..78 to50..58 to remain above the raised weapon (top row60).
- `tools/bake.braw` adds HUD32x32 support while preserving sprite/weapon sizes;
  `tools/bake_polish.braw` bakes the arriving sources. All eight faces and both
  64x96 fist frames arrived before completion, were inspected, baked to the
  game palette and added to BAKE_ASSETS. All10 headers and payload sizes pass.
  Source PNGs were never edited here. Optional labelled fallbacks remain in the
  loader for an incomplete asset checkout, but the final asset set is complete.
