  `.../scratchpad/w/gate-cargo-capped.sh`, link guard `.../scratchpad/w/ld-guard.sh`.
- Native default build must stay byte-for-byte as it was (host target unchanged
  unless the new flag is given). Existing replays/hashes unchanged.

  replay hashes of the native build (headless shell) — that is the oracle.
