# Bogle Castle stage 4

Arthur requested E1M1 playable start to exit with the four v1 weapons, four
v1 enemies, keys, pickups and hazards. The work is complete and uncommitted.
The lead owns git. No external posts, dependency changes or cargo clean ran.

## Implementation

Stage 3's game.braw only advanced player movement and rendered static map
things. game.braw:9 now initializes gameplay state; game.braw:69 advances
weapons, projectiles, pickups, hazards, doors and AI from replay input alone.
The optional new_game(map, aye) enables focused maps with only enemies/doors;
old renderer maps keep their static doors and existing stage 2 hashes.

- engine/things.braw:4 creates mutable actors; :60 handles spirit/health;
  :76 damage and haggis chains; :119 shared collision and grounded stepdowns;
  :141 pickups; :199 hazards and bounded seeded bog spawns.
- engine/ai.braw:32 implements keeper patrol/windup/charge/wall stun, dormant
  haggis pursuit/contact, ghost wall passage/light vulnerability/wails and
  midge movement/contact damage. Contact and splash check portal visibility.
- engine/weapons.braw:7 handles nearest hitscan; :31 the exact cone/range
  bagpipe blast; :86 projectiles, gravity, one bounce, splash and sizzling;
  :151 weapon charge, ammo and cooldown. PRNG lives in fixed.braw.
- engine/doors.braw:3 initializes moving ceiling doors; :54 implements
  directional use, iron/silver locks, safe closing and the exit. move.braw's
  common portal trace supplies vertical sight and inclusive door-use endpoints.
- maps/e1m1.braw builds 43 convex sectors through courtyard, gatehouse, hall,
  kitchen, eight descending steps, crypt, gallery, chapel and battlements.
  data/e1m1.map is generated by that builder. The two key gates are ordered.
- shell/frame.braw defaults to E1M1; Space/left mouse fires, E uses, 1-4 selects.
  The existing fixed-tic accumulator, recording and host-owned loop remain.
- assets/render add dynamic enemies, pickups, projectiles, dressing, weapon
  frames, HUD and messages. Mars sizzle uses the baked low splat. Weapon
  flashes last five tics rather than hiding the scene throughout cooldown.
- tools/bake_all.braw generated 67 BOG outputs including colormap. Magenta
  dominance (r,b>=128 and r-g,b-g>=64) removes sprite/weapon fringes. Textures
  and sky keep the previous alpha/near-magenta predicate; all 11 preexisting
  texture/sky files match the original bytes. Raw BOGs have binary attributes.

The engine purity scanner passes: no floats, bridge, clock or random builtins.
Randomness is the seeded Park-Miller generator stored in each game state.

on native MDH_RC=0 and MDH_RC=1; both assert exit reached alive, both keys and
all four weapons owned, and match all five hashes including the final frame.
The debug interpreter runs the first 140 tics and matches that prefix hash.
No JS/WASM game execution is claimed; the web game is stage 8.

asserts midge health0/inactive and keeper stunned timer52, damage and positive
knockback, rather than relying on a frame hash.

visual_gameplay. Existing bake fixtures also gained fringe/opaque-purple checks.

hazards, weapon damage/ammo, bounce, cone/range/LOS, lock/door/exit behavior,
