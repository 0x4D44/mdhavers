# Bogle Castle: the story campaign (HLD)

Status: SELF-APPROVED (2L flow, 2026-11-01). Not SIGNED-OFF: Arthur's gate is integration.

## 1. Goal

Turn Bogle Castle from two unconnected key-hunt levels into one comic story campaign:
rescue Princess Ailsa from Laird Angus McDreichBottom. The tone is Day of the Tentacle
x Doom x Portal: Doom-style fighting, adventure-game puzzles and inventory gags, and a
deadpan lab computer. Source: Arthur's story (2026-11-01 14:48) and decisions (14:54).

**In scope.** Five campaign levels, the engine features they need, new actors and art,
endings chosen by time to save Ailsa, per-level replay oracles, the web build working.

**Out of scope.** Save games, difficulty levels, voice acting, new music (levels reuse
the two existing tunes), the Windows bridge, publishing to 0x4d44.github.io (separate
go), any language or compiler change.

**Endings.** The campaign clock counts every gameplay tic of every attempt, restarts
and deaths included, exactly once. It stops when Ailsa is freed (the third lever):

- freed before `END_FAST` -> "Ye mairry Ailsa. Ye hae seeven bairns. Yin o them is a clone."
- freed before `END_SLOW` -> "Ye mairry Ailsa. Ye hae nae bairns, but a verra guid personality core."
- freed later, or the countdown runs out -> "Auld Wullie buries ye in the kirkyard. He
  didnae check if ye were deid." The fail ending.

The ending card shows after the core is fitted (or at once on countdown expiry) with
the time and Back to Title. Thresholds are tuned once the routes exist (D6).

**Death** in any level keeps Restart and Back to Title, with the line "HERE LIES A
GIRDER JUICE SALESMAN" (D5).

## 3. Approach

The engine stays pure (no host calls, no floats, seeded randomness only), so every new
feature replays to identical results on the interpreter, native RC off/on and wasm.
Maps without a `story` record (E1M1, E1M2, test maps) behave exactly as today.

### 3.1 Campaign and the clock (shell)

- `bogle_levels()` becomes the five campaign maps. `e1m1.map` and `e1m2.map` stay with
  their tests as regression maps (D1, D3).
- Carry gains `items` (story items, for example `"head"`) and `clock`. A new
  `bogle_apply_carry(g, carry)` in a shared shell file is the only way carry enters a
  game; the front end and the headless runner both use it.
- **Clock accounting.** `g["clock"]` starts at the carried value and increments on every
  `tic`, talk tics included. Leaving a level by winning, Restart, Restart Level or Back
  to Title banks the attempt: the front end keeps `ui["clock"]`, sets it from the game
  as it leaves, and restarts carry it in. The clock is frozen once Ailsa is freed.
- Headings: maps with a `story` record use their `name` ("THE KIRKYARD COMPLETE!");
  maps without one keep the hardcoded Gatehouse strings, so their hashes stay unchanged.
- New `ending` screen (tier text, time, Back to Title). `g["failed"]` (countdown) stops
  `tic` like `won` and sends the front end to the fail ending.
- The opening card is a front-end screen before the first briefing (any key continues).

### 3.2 Story scripts (new engine/story.braw)

Per-level behaviour is braw code, not a map scripting language (D2). A map names its
script with a `story <id>` record. `story.braw` dispatches on the id:

- `story_init(g)`: objective line, level flags (`g["story"]`, a dict), map overrides.
- `story_event(g, tag)`: called from `bogle_activate` for each link tag that fires.
- `story_tick(g)`: once per tic after actors (countdown, clone, meter value).
- `story_use(g, actor)`: E on a story thing (types 40-59) within 64 units with sight.
  **A fresh E press goes to the nearest usable story thing first and is consumed**;
  only if none is in reach does it fall through to switches, doors and the exit (M4).

Puzzle state is the script's: the organ keys, compressor and levers are story things
(not switch walls), so the script owns order, reset, idempotence and the on/off sprite.
The crypt door and scanner door have their own link tags, which only the script sends
via `bogle_activate` after validating (A5, S4). A lever already pulled does nothing.

Helpers: `story_say(g, lines)`, `story_ask(g, lines, choices, event)`,
`story_objective(g, text)`, `story_give(g, item)`, `story_win(g)`, `story_fail(g)`.
`g["objective"]` replaces `bogle_hud_objective` when set.

### 3.3 Talk box (engine)

`g["talk"] = {"lines", "choices", "sel", "event"}`. **The freeze sits at the top of
`tic`**, before turning, weapon selection and firing: while talk is open, only the clock
advances and only Use (fresh press) and forward/back (fresh press) are read. Use
advances or chooses. Opening and closing talk clears `bag_charge`, sets `was_use` and
`was_fire` to the current input, and sets `g["hold_fire"]` so Fire does nothing until it
is released (no gun or pipe blast leaks out of talk; S5, S13). Drawn as a box in the
lower third: up to 4 word-wrapped lines in the 3x5 font, choices with a `>` marker.
Choosing sends `event` to `story_event`.

### 3.4 Kinds, locks and items

- Predicates replace every hardcoded range (S2, S3): `bogle_is_monster(kind)` (2..5,
  42..45, 49), `bogle_is_pickup(kind)` (10..20, 22..24) and `bogle_is_story_thing(kind)`
  (40..59), used in damage, hitscan, projectiles, splash, the bagpipe cone, AI dispatch,
  door and lift blocking, curling, render height/texture and pickup messages (a dict by
  kind, not `messages[kind-10]`). Old kinds give identical answers, so E1M1/E1M2 hashes
  stay unchanged. Protected actors (Wullie, Ailsa, cloaks, clone) are hit by weapons
  but take no damage; Wullie says "Ye cannae shoot Wullie!".
- Locks: 3 = brass key (key bit 4), 4 = retina, needs item `head`. Locked doors carry no
  link tag (the code checks links first). Pickups: 22 brass key, 23 the head, 24 the
  good core. Path points (type 60) are stored in `g["paths"]`, not as actors (M3).
- Ailsa's spanner is a projectile owned by an actor that can hit only the contraption.

**Ghosts and light** (S6, S7): the ghost's hurt rule reads the light of its current
sector, and ghosts drift anywhere. Story maps may set `ghostlight <n>` (default 96);
kirkyard and forest set it below their dimmest moonlit sector, so ghosts are always
hurtable there, and a unit test checks that every sector of those maps is above it.

### 3.6 The seen-o-meter (tower)

A pure function of `tics` and the distance to the nearest lit-doorway sector, drawn as
a big red gauge with an alarm cue at the peak. It reads game state and writes nothing:
a unit test runs the same input with the meter on and off and compares game state.

### 3.7 Countdown

The computer's choice starts a 3-minute countdown shown on the HUD. The third lever
stops it and the clock. Expiry calls `story_fail`.

### 3.8 Maps

New builder scripts in `maps/` for `kirkyard`, `forest`, `tower`, `lab`, in the e1m2
cell-collector style, sky ceilings outdoors. `distillery` adds the landing to the E1M2
rooms; the shared parts move into a helper used by both, and `data/e1m2.map` must
regenerate byte-identical (M5). The landing is a helper (`maps/landing.braw`) used by
`distillery` (scanner refuses) and `lab` (rubble left, scanner accepts the head): a
story-changed room is two authored maps, not runtime geometry (D2).

### 3.9 Art

The existing Codex image pipeline (design 4.4). Batches per level, each reviewed on its
own: (1) Wullie with lantern (idle, walk 2, rotations), grave, kirk stone, stained
glass, organ console, organ keys off/on, compressor off/on, crypt door, lamp post,
brass key, sheet music; (2) deer (idle, walk 2, charge, pain, death), thistle tuft and
pop 3, tree standing, falling 2, fallen, bark and hedge walls, earth tunnel, signposts,
bell, grandfather clock; (3) rat (idle, walk 2, death), rubble, scanner door; (4) green
cloak (2), clone (idle, sparks), head pickup, panelled corridor; (5) Ailsa (strapped,
free, throw), contraption (idle, walk 2, blast, vent, slumped), computer, levers, core.

## 5. Oracles

- **Per level:** an autopilot route (as `tools/route_e1m2.braw`) from a committed carry
  fixture `replays/<level>.carry` applied through `bogle_apply_carry`, completing the level
  with `BOGLE_REQUIRE_EXIT=1` and the map's `require` record. Native RC off/on equal the
  committed hashes; the interpreter prefix (140 tics) agrees.
- **State digest** (A4): for story maps each hash row adds an FNV digest of a canonical
  story-state string (health, keys, items, story flags, talk, countdown, clock, actor
  states). Legacy maps print exactly what they print today.
- **Campaign:** a native-only frontend replay through all five levels and the ending
  (the routes chained), with an interpreter prefix; plus a fast unit fixture that drives
  `bogle_front_won` through every transition and checks map order, carry and tiers (C1).
- `BOGLE_REQUIRE_EXIT=1` on a map without `require` keeps the current check (M1).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. New Game: opening card -> kirkyard -> forest -> distillery -> tower -> lab -> ending,
   through the native campaign replay, equal to its committed hashes on RC off and on.
2. Each campaign level's route completes it alive from its carry fixture, with native
   RC off/on and interpreter-prefix agreement against committed hashes and digests.
3. E1M1, E1M2, stage2, stage3 and frontend replays and hashes are unchanged; `data/e1m2.map`
   regenerates byte-identical; existing fixtures pass, or are re-blessed only where they
   pin a deliberate change, listed in the journal with the reason.
4. Unit fixtures, each made to fail once:
   - talk freezes the world (positions and `tics` unchanged, clock advances); no shot or
     pipe blast leaks out when talk closes; choices need fresh presses;
   - every choice at the computer leads to "INTERPRETING VAGUE ANSWER AS NO." and the
     countdown; expiry gives the fail ending;
   - clock: death -> Restart, pause -> Restart Level, Back to Title -> New Game and
     repeated restarts each bank every tic once; tier boundaries on both sides;
   - new monsters are damaged by every weapon (hitscan, Mars bar splash, blunderbuss,
     bagpipes) and block doors; protected actors take no damage;
   - Wullie: sees inside range and cone after the grace, not behind or beyond; drains
     only while seeing;
   - organ: nothing before the compressor ("Nae air"); right order opens the crypt; wrong
     note resets; repeated notes and repeated lever pulls are idempotent;
   - thistle tuft, pop and prod; tree falls on proximity and on standing still, hurts
     only on its line, once;
   - the meter changes nothing;
   - the clone drops the head; the scanner refuses without it and opens with it;
   - Ailsa stays in the arena and hurts only the contraption; the contraption vents,
     slumps and wins only with `good_core`;
   - every sector of kirkyard and forest is above its `ghostlight`.
5. `bogle_unit` purity scan green.
6. The web page builds from the branch and the kirkyard route under node equals its
   native hashes.
